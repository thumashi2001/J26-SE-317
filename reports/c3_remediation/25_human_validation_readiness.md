## Limits

- The approval register is a record kept by people, not a proof. The software cannot authenticate the approver, check that they are authorised, or verify the evidence reference; it only checks that the record is complete, names no individual, and matches the rubric content it was made for.
- A passing audit shows that records are complete and consistent. It is not research validity and not operational security.

## Reproduce

From `ai-services/c3`: `python -m datasets.gold.readiness datasets/c3_phase3_v5_repaired_v2 --rubrics-dir <dir> --approvals <register> --markdown -` (with no rubric directory every question is reported missing).
